Lesson 2 · Hello and goodbye

Greetings, times of the day, thanks.

1Hello

Russian has several ways to say «hello». The difference is who you talk to.
Приве́т!
Hi! (informal, to one person)
Hola
Всем приве́т!
Hello, everyone! (friends)
Hola a todos
Здра́вствуй!
Hello (half-formal, to one person)
Hola (más formal)
Здра́вствуйте!
Hello (formal, to one person or a group)
Buenos días (formal)
Ladder of politeness:Приве́т (friends) → Здра́вствуй (half-formal) → Здра́вствуйте (formal). все = everyone, so «Всем приве́т!» = «Hi, everybody!»
When you are not sure, use «Здра'вствуйте». It is always safe.
Quick check
You meet a good friend. You say:
A friend gets the informal «Приве́т».
Quick check
You meet your professor. You say:
A teacher or a stranger gets the formal «Здра́вствуйте».
Quick check
You walk into a room full of your friends. You say:
«Всем» = to all of them. Friends, so informal.
Quick check
What does this mean?
Здра́вствуйте = formal hello.

2Times of the day

До́брое у́тро!
Good morning!
Buenos días
До́брый день!
Good afternoon!
Buenas tardes
До́брый ве́чер!
Good evening!
Buenas noches (al llegar)
Споко́йной но́чи!
Good night! (more popular)
Buenas noches (al irse)
До́брой но́чи!
Good night!
Buenas noches
Why different endings?Words have gender. утро is neuter → «до́брое». день, вечер are masculine → «до́брый». ночь is feminine → «до́брой».
Quick check
Which picture means «Good morning»?
утро = morning.
Quick check
Which picture means «Good evening»?
вечер = evening.
Quick check
Which picture means «Good night»?
ночь = night. «Споко́йной но́чи» is the most popular.
Quick check
What does this mean?
день = day.
Quick check
До́бр___ у́тро!
Fill the gap: «Добр___ утро!»
утро is neuter, so «до́брое».

3Goodbye and thanks

Пока́!
Bye! (informal)
Chao
До свида́ния!
Goodbye! (formal)
Adiós / Hasta la vista
Спаси́бо!
Thank you!
Gracias
Пожа́луйста.
Please. / You're welcome.
Por favor / De nada
Inside «до свидания»:до = until, свидание = a date, a meeting (встреча = a meeting). Literally: «until the next meeting».
Quick check
You leave a shop. You say to the seller:
A stranger gets the formal goodbye.
Quick check
You leave your friend's house. You say:
Friends: «Пока́».
Quick check
What does this mean?
Спаси́бо = thank you.
Quick check
Someone says «Спасибо!» to you. You answer:
Пожа́луйста = you're welcome.
