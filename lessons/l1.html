Lesson 1 · Alphabet and reading

All 33 letters and the five reading rules.

1Welcome

This is your Russian course, step by step. Every lesson explains a topic first, then gives you quick checks on exactly that topic. Do them right away, while the idea is fresh. Cada lección explica un tema y luego te da pruebas rápidas.
1 · Read
Look at the picture and the phrase.
2 · Listen
Tap any card and it speaks. Slow audio is in Settings.
3 · Say it
Say it out loud before the check.
4 · Check
Mistakes are fine. They are training.
Your Spanish helps.Russian vowels are clean like Spanish, the Russian «р» is rolled like Spanish «rr», and «х» is the Spanish «j». Spanish hints can be switched on or off in Settings.

6Rule 1: stress

The rule:In every Russian word one vowel is stressed (louder and longer). We show it with a mark: вода́. An unstressed «о» sounds like «а».

Always find the stress first. Then read.
Quick check
вода́
How do you read this word?
Stress on the last syllable. The unstressed «о» sounds like «а».
Quick check
молоко́
How do you read this word?
Only the last «о» is stressed. The other two sound like «а».
Quick check
окно́
How do you read this word?
окно́ = window.

7Rule 2: soft vowels

The rule:я, ю, е, ё, и make the letter before them soft. «ня» sounds like Spanish «ña», «ню» like «ñu». The soft sign ь does the same and has no sound.

8Rule 3: the end of a word

The rule:At the end of a word, a voiced consonant gets quieter: б → п, в → ф, г → к, д → т, ж → ш, з → с. We spell it one way and say it the other.
